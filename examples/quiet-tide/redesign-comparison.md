## What changed

- Replaced generated shading with three deliberate SVG sources: opaque backdrop, sun, and wave.
- Simplified the wave into one stable silhouette with a deliberate eye-shaped counterform.
- Reduced incidental texture and highlight noise.
- Let Icon Composer own the platform mask, glass edge, material response, and appearance previews.
- Preserved the core recognition equation: a calm wave embracing a sun.

The reconstruction is intentionally not a pixel copy. Its job is to preserve identity while making every curve, layer, and material decision editable and reproducible.

## Evidence boundary

- **Validated:** real Icon Composer document, exported 1024 px Default PNG, iOS 26.5 Simulator build and Home Screen rendering.
- **Prepared:** reusable SVG source layers and an XcodeGen fixture.
- **Not tested:** physical device, tvOS, visionOS, App Review, or conversion outcomes.
