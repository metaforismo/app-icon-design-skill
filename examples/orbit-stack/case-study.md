# Orbit Stack

![Orbit Stack concept](concept-master.png)

## Brief

- Fictional product: spatial notes
- Promise: organize layered ideas in motion
- Direction: Liquid Glass system
- Recognition anchor: three diamonds crossed by one lime spark

## Why it works

The repeated diamond creates rhythm and depth, while the acid-lime spark supplies a small, stable identity anchor. The concept naturally maps to a limited number of z-groups.

## Risks

- Stacked layers can resemble generic database or design-tool symbols.
- The generated orbit line contradicts the prompt’s avoid list and should be removed.
- The top and middle layers merge when small.
- Strong glow and frosted edges are baked into the concept bitmap.

## Production reconstruction

Use four groups:

1. background fill in Icon Composer
2. lower diamond
3. middle and upper diamonds, split only if independent depth is necessary
4. opaque spark accent

Remove the orbit line entirely. Offset the diamonds enough to preserve three levels at 32 px. Use Icon Composer refraction selectively and keep the lime spark opaque.
