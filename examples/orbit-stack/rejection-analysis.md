# Orbit Stack: rejected production direction

![Orbit Stack concept](concept-master.png)

Orbit Stack remains useful as a concept-study example, but this exact direction is rejected for production.

## Why it was rejected

1. The three translucent plates merge into a low-contrast stack at small sizes.
2. The orbit stroke becomes too thin before the central motif becomes distinctive.
3. “Stacked layers plus a spark” is a crowded visual category and does not yet create an ownable silhouette.
4. Its appeal depends heavily on blue glass, glow, and specular polish; the identity weakens in Mono and Tinted appearances.
5. Reconstructing the bitmap faithfully would preserve the wrong problem: generic geometry rather than a durable recognition anchor.

## A better next experiment

Keep only the spatial-note promise. Replace the literal stack with one asymmetric folded plane and a strong cutout. Test the mark first in one color at 32 px, then add at most one controlled glass layer.

- **Validated:** static 1024 px concept and audit artifacts.
